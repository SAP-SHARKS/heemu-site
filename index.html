Permissions

How Heemu uses the Accessibility Service.

Heemu asks for one sensitive Android permission. Here is exactly what it does, and what it does not.

Why it is needed

Heemu reads the name of the app that is currently open on your screen. This is the only way an app can tell when you open another app, and it is what makes the mindful pause possible. Without it, Heemu cannot know when to step in.

What it reads

Only the name of the app in the foreground, so it can be checked against the list of apps you picked yourself.

What it never reads

Your messages, your passwords, anything you type, or the content of any screen. Heemu does not record or capture your screen.

Where it goes

Nowhere. The app name is checked on your device and is never collected, stored on our servers, or shared with anyone.

Your control

Heemu is not an accessibility tool and is not built for users with disabilities. The service is never switched on by itself. You are asked inside the app first, you choose whether to agree, and you turn it on yourself in Android Settings. You can turn it off at any time, and Heemu keeps working without the pause feature.

Heemu also asks for Usage Access to show your daily screen time. That one is optional. Mindful pauses work without it, only the statistics will be missing. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is Heemu free?

Yes. Heemu is free to download and use, with no ads and no data selling. We built it as a sadaqah jariyah for the Ummah.

Does Heemu track what I do on my phone?

No. Heemu only detects when an app you have personally chosen to limit opens, and that check never leaves your phone. We do not read your screen, your messages, or your keystrokes, and we do not use analytics or tracking of any kind. Your dhikr and ayat counts are saved to your account so your progress survives a reinstall. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Why does the app need Accessibility permission?

Android's Accessibility Service is the only way for an app to detect when another app opens. We use it strictly to show you a mindful pause when you open a distracting app.

It reads only the name of the app in front, so Heemu can check it against the list you picked yourself. It does not read your messages, your passwords, or anything you type, and it never records or captures your screen. Nothing read through this service is saved, sent to our servers, or shared with anyone.

You choose which apps trigger a pause, you switch the service on yourself in Android Settings, and you can revoke it at any time. Full details here.

Will this work on iPhone?

We're currently focused on Android. iOS has stricter limitations due to Apple's policies. We're exploring an iOS version using Screen Time APIs. Sign up for updates to be first to know.

Can I choose which apps to limit?

Yes. You have full control. During setup you choose exactly which apps trigger the intervention. You can change this list anytime in Settings.

What if I really need to use a limited app?

After the brief pause you can always choose to continue. Heemu is a gentle guide, not a strict prison.

Who made Heemu?

Heemu is built by Uhud Labs LLC, a small team of Muslim developers who believe technology should bring us closer to Allah, not farther. You can reach us at support@uhudlabs.com.
